## Sales Performance Analysis and Dashboard Using Excel

## Project Overview
For this project, I worked with sales data covering January to May 2025 to understand how the business was performing across its products, sales representatives, and cities.
My focus was on looking beyond total sales to understand where the profit was coming from, which products contributed the most to business performance, how revenue was distributed across sales representatives, and how costs differed across cities.
I used Microsoft Excel to analyze the data, calculate key performance indicators, create PivotTables and charts, and bring the results together in a sales performance dashboard.
The dashboard provides a summary of the business's performance and makes it easier to identify important figures without having to go through the entire dataset.

## The Business Questions I Wanted to Answer
While working on the project, I focused on the following questions:
- How much revenue and profit did the business generate?
- What was the overall profit margin?
- Which products contributed the most to total profit?
- How much of the business's profit came from its top-performing products?
- Which sales representative generated the highest revenue?
- How did the cost of goods sold compare across cities?
- How did recorded customer activity change from month to month?
- What should the business investigate further based on these findings?
These questions guided the analysis and helped me focus on findings that would be useful to someone making business decisions.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
I used Excel to organize and analyze the sales data, calculate KPIs, summarize performance using PivotTables, and build the dashboard.
Other Excel features used in the project include:
- Formulas for calculating and evaluating business metrics
- PivotTables for summarizing sales and profit data
- PivotCharts for presenting the results visually
- Slicers for filtering dashboard information
  
## How I Approached the Project
I started by working with the available sales dataset and identifying the measures needed to answer the business questions.
I then calculated the key performance indicators and used PivotTables to summarize the data across relevant categories, including products and cities. These summaries helped me compare performance and identify the strongest contributors.
After analyzing the results, I developed the Excel dashboard to bring the main KPIs and visual findings together in one place. I also incorporated filtering functionality so the data could be explored from different perspectives.
The final stage was interpreting the numbers and identifying what they could mean for the business, rather than presenting charts without explaining their relevance.

 ## Analysis and Key Findings

# 1. Revenue, Profit and Profit Margin: The business generated approximately ₦2.33 billion in revenue and ₦465.67 million in profit. This translates to a profit margin of approximately 20%. I included these figures to establish a clear picture of the business's overall financial performance before looking at individual products and sales contributors.

# 2.  Product Profitability: Laptop A13 was the highest-profit product, generating approximately ₦105.3 million in profit. This accounted for about 23% of the business's total profit.The analysis also showed that Laptop A13, Sofa Classic, and Desktop PC D21 together contributed approximately 52% of total profit.This was an important finding because it showed that a relatively small group of products accounted for a substantial share of the business's profit.

# 3.  Sales Representative Performance: Peter generated approximately ₦434.8 million in revenue, representing about 19% of the total revenue generated by the six sales representatives.This helped identify the highest-revenue contributor within the sales team.

# 4.  Cost of Goods Sold Across Cities: Lagos recorded the highest cost of goods sold at approximately ₦493.8 million, while Abuja recorded the lowest at approximately ₦423.3 million. The difference was approximately ₦70.5 million, meaning Lagos's total COGS was about 17% higher than Abuja's. This comparison identifies a difference worth investigating, but it does not automatically mean that Lagos is less efficient. Higher costs could be associated with higher sales volumes or differences in the products sold.

# 5.   Monthly Customer Activity: Between January and April 2025, the recorded monthly customer counts ranged from 499 to 536, with April recording the highest count. The figures remained within a relatively narrow range during these four months, suggesting that recorded activity was fairly consistent over the period. May contained only 18 records in the dataset, so I treated it as a partial month rather than comparing it directly with the preceding months.

## Recommendations Based on the Analysis
- Review the top-performing products: Monitor the profitability and availability of Laptop A13, Sofa Classic, and Desktop PC D21 because they account for a substantial share of total profit.
- Investigate product-level margins: Compare revenue, COGS, and profit across products to understand which products generate the strongest returns.
- Look beyond sales revenue when evaluating representatives: Include profit contribution and transaction activity to build a more complete picture of sales performance.
- Continue monitoring monthly activity: Use complete reporting periods to identify meaningful changes in recorded customer or transaction activity.
 These recommendations are based on the observations in the available data.
